# Satellite Card

![Screaming into space](item:ocr_extra_drivers:satellitecard)

The satellite card is an upgraded [network card](../../item/lancard.md) that can send and receive network messages between ground and orbit. Any satellite card on the ground can receive messages sent by one in orbit, but not from other satellite cards on the ground.

For the message to reach the orbit/ground, the way must be clear. The signal can travel through glass and some small blocks. The satellite card consumes more energy than a [wireless card](../../item/wlancard1.md), but much less than a [linked card](../../item/linkedcard.md).

When used in a relay, you may want to disable repeating.

Supported space mods:
- Ad Astra
- Stellaris
